What is ATS and why your resume needs to pass it

Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) are used by 98% of Fortune 500 companies. They scan resumes for keywords, formatting, and section structure before a human sees it. If your resume lacks the right keywords or uses complex layouts, it gets rejected — even if you’re the perfect fit.

How ATS works

ATS parses your resume into fields: contact info, work experience, skills, education. It then matches those against the job description. Our AI simulator replicates this process locally.

Why resumes get rejected

Missing keywords (up to 60% drop), no skills section, poor formatting (tables/columns), or missing contact details. This tool highlights exactly what’s missing.

Importance of keyword matching

Recruiters search for specific terms like “Python” or “project management”. Our tool compares your resume text with the job description and gives you a match ratio.
